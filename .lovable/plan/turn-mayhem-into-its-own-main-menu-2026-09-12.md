# Turn MAYHEM into its own main menu

## Main menu
- Replace the teaser-style MAYHEM screen controls with a dedicated mode title screen over the existing artwork, rain, and atmosphere.
- Add three prominent controls: `COMMENCE`, `SETTINGS`, and `WATCH THIS BEFORE PLAYING.`
- Keep the YouTube control visibly unavailable until its link is supplied.

## Behavior
- `COMMENCE` starts the existing MAYHEM intro dialogue as the current first step into the mode.
- `SETTINGS` opens a MAYHEM-styled settings panel using the game's existing audio and accessibility settings.
- Preserve rain audio, mouse parallax, and returning to other main-menu tabs.

- Confirm all three controls render clearly on desktop and mobile landscape.
