# MAYHEM rules

- Office atmosphere owns filtered storm sources separately from shared outdoor SFX, follows live SFX volume, ducks only beneath audible powered music, and disposes sources on exit; visual layers stay below controls and honor reduced effects/motion.

- NightIntro owns the post-tip static/text/reveal sequence; pure timing rules gate exit on asset readiness while NightRooms keeps gameplay inactive until reveal ends.
- NightIntro signals the page-level audio owner the instant its text appears so the night-start sting stays locked to the picture, and tested `nightLookActive` lets the room's smoothed gaze run from the moment the card mounts while gameplay stays frozen.

- Receptionist merchant dialogue cues use an isolated overlay that owns shop audio; session upgrades are passed from the page into NightRooms and pure merchant rules, keeping effects testable and enemy activation deferred.
- DialogueBox reports its active line to external character artwork; merchant expression assignments live alongside its script so artwork changes stay synchronized without duplicating dialogue progression.
- Generator control hover cues are delegated from its puzzle container and use the shared night SFX bus with cached crushed samples; this covers every puzzle without changing terminal Flow audio.
- Generator-only music uses an isolated audio lifecycle with a pure progress-to-track/rate rule and displayed progress as its clock; start both loops on one audio-clock boundary with matching rate and loop length, switch their gains rather than restart, and derive button beats from that audio clock. Duck the night soundtrack while audible and release all sources on dismissal so timing, pause, and room audio stay independent.
- MAYHEM night-clear cinematics own their media lifecycle and derive picture beats and the shared sound/picture fade from the completion audio clock; separate descent and pulse transform layers preserve both motions, while the page prepares the next available floor and keeps gameplay frozen until reveal finishes.
- Generator progress gains use a pure helper fed by the active night captured in NightRooms, keeping all puzzle types consistent and the completion cap testable.
- Generator double-charge rewards use a pure helper with an explicit random roll and persist the bonus flag in owned progress; award feedback reads that flag and routes the boss parry sample through the night bus so muted platformer audio cannot suppress it.
- Generator completion feedback animates displayed progress independently from owned progress, starting layered impact audio with the reward and count audio on the rise clock; final night completion waits for the rise.
- NightRooms drives generator and flip-tab look offsets from the existing smoothed gaze through separate transform layers, preserving independent flip and reward animations.
- MAYHEM room artwork is angularly re-projected by the isolated Three.js `CurvedRoom` renderer, with shared smoothed gaze and DOM fallback; this makes head turns visibly curved without changing authored paintings or gameplay.
- CurvedRoom converts pointer coordinates into shader angles through tested `roomLookAngles` rules, keeping screen-space and projection-axis signs consistent.
- Room changes darken the outgoing view before swapping artwork and reveal the incoming view during a directional sweep; the shared gaze stays frame-rate independent and panel pointers remain ordinary.
- MAYHEM NPC reactions are triggered by page-level dialogue completion and animated in canvas runtime state, keeping story timing aligned with world visuals.
- MAYHEM night puzzle and terminal-error progress is owned by `NightRooms` and passed into overlays, so lowering a panel never resets an active repair.- MAYHEM night threats live on the 12×12 grid in `useGridRoster` (roster config + per-night move chance), owned by `NightRooms` so movement continues while the map is closed.
- MAYHEM hallway threats (Nicky/AN-B) use pure timing rules in `encounterRules.ts`; NightRooms owns encounter state and grid `inHall` flags from `useGridRoster`, so outcomes stay testable and pause-safe.
- Night 5 Manager's Office test room (`ManagerOffice.tsx`, toggled by "\") uses flat 2D wall pictures slid by the same yaw/pitch camera curves (no 3D warping), with pure look/task rules in `managerOfficeRules.ts`, kept separate from NightRooms so threats and the boss can be added later without touching Nights 1–4.
- Night 5 desk console tasks (power, code, Simon, wires, surge) run in `DeskConsole.tsx` with pure progression in `officeConsoleRules.ts`; ManagerOffice owns progress so closing the console never resets a task. Power and code tasks use the bundled Stevenson generator sprite. Night 5 task completions never play the Nights 1-4 generator reward impact; only the physical plug seating does.
- Night 5's door-close SFX is bundled locally and plays only when the door transitions from open to closed, from either keyboard or pointer input.
- ANYDROID-B's Night 5 office encounter is triggered once by 3; her teleport transitions directly to the landing pose, and attack-pose movement/light-on-head sounds are owned by `ManagerOffice`. Pure rules own the 1.5s landing, 2.4s stand, random attack poses, 200ms head-target flashlight damage, and GAH-synchronized full-screen defeat shake/stretch/fade. Sprite backgrounds are transparent and local.
- `useOfficeMusic` suspends its office-owned loop whenever inactive; the Night 5 death path stops shared BGM so menu music cannot leak into the death or restart sequence.
- Desk Console code, Simon, wire, and surge controls use a shared procedural microswitch click; wire connections are sagging SVG curves with pointer-following held cables and a brief success jiggle, while task buttons and ports use physical pressed states.
- NEO-KID's test laser is triggered by Digit 2, plays three supplied warning cues together before a four-shot salvo, randomly selects a duration-preserving pitch variant for each beam, locks each shot to the current cursor Y, and uses pure telegraph/blast/hit-band timing rules.
- NEO-MATA's office visit (crawl, vent, shocked) and vent air live in ManagerOffice with pure drain/rattle/shock-duration rules in `managerOfficeRules.ts`, so pacing stays testable; Task 1 runs on the Stevenson artwork in DeskConsole and office music waits for its completion.
- The Manager's Office death rules keep The Gifted's 4.2s door-close, 0.5s reopen, and 3.5s safe-wait timings in `managerOfficeRules.ts`; register each death clip and its MP3 soundtrack together in `officeDeathVideos.ts`. `OfficeDeathScreen` keeps any aspect ratio contained in the viewport, mutes video audio, synchronizes the compatible MP3 to playback, and remounts a fresh run when it ends. Empty NEO-MATA air starts a red flash with the trimmed death cue, then cuts to black before the same death video.
